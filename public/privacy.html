Privacy

Slow Windows, the web app and the Android app. Last updated 17 October 2026.

Slow Windows is free and has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect, sell or share personal information. Everything you set up stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, favourites, focus sessions, scene choices and listening history are kept in the app's own storage on your device. They are never sent anywhere. "Back up" in the app saves them to a file only you handle.
  • Music and photos you add are copied into the app's storage on your device. They are never uploaded.
  • Music you download for offline listening, and pictures you save of a scene, stay on your device.

What the app fetches, and from where

To work, the app downloads files from these services. Like any website, each sees your device's internet address when it is asked for something; none is given anything else about you.

  • GitHub Pages (danieljcroberts.github.io), where the app itself, its scene art and some of its music are hosted. The Android app also checks here for a newer version.
  • The Internet Archive (archive.org), which hosts most of the Creative Commons music the app streams.
  • Google Fonts, for the app's typefaces.
  • NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center (services.swpc.noaa.gov), for the aurora forecast shown in the aurora scenes. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), only if you turn on "Use my location": your position, rounded to about a kilometre, is sent to get the local weather and sunrise times. It is kept only on your device, and turning the option off removes it.

Android permissions

  • Notifications: the playing track with its controls, and the end of a focus block.
  • Run in the foreground and keep the device awake while playing: so music keeps playing with the screen off.
  • Alarms: the sunrise alarm, which opens the app at the time you choose.
  • Approximate location: only when you turn on "Use my location", for local weather and sunrise times.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): to save a picture of a scene to your photos.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.
