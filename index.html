About and credits

  





  
Welcome to Slow Windows. Press Play for music. Swipe sideways for scenes; tap for the controls, or near the bottom for a mini player. Double-tap: left = previous, middle = play/pause, right = next. Slide the right edge for volume. Tap the scene's name, or long-press, for all scenes; ⋯ holds everything else.

  




  
Music

  
Your own music, kept on this device, and Creative Commons tracks: the ones that appeared in the Music For Programming mixes (episode numbers show where), plus more from the same artists' releases. For those, credit the artist, keep the app non-commercial for NC licences, and play ND tracks unedited.
