                About and credits
                
  
                

                

                
  
                Welcome to Slow Windows. Pick a mood to start, or press Play for music. Swipe sideways for scenes; tap for the controls, or near the bottom for a mini player. Double-tap: left = previous, middle = play/pause, right = next. Slide the right edge for volume. Tap the scene's name, or long-press, for all scenes; ⋯ holds everything else.
